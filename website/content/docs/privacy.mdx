---
title: Privacy Policy
description: How Boop handles identities, messages, calls, and device data.
---

Boop is an Android and iOS messenger developed by Oriel Haim. Your device manages your Nostr identity. Independent relays and other services carry traffic. Boop has no developer-operated account, messaging, or calling backend and includes no developer-operated analytics, advertising, or crash reporting.

## Identity and local data

Boop creates or imports your key on the device and keeps it in the operating system's secure key storage. Your public key is your shareable address. Profile fields you publish are public Nostr data. Local contacts, nicknames, history, drafts, call records, preferences, and downloaded files stay in app storage. Call history can include participants, times, duration, and outcome.

Digital SIM exports a passphrase-encrypted copy of your identity key. You choose where to save it. Recovering the key alone restores your address. Conversation recovery depends on Marmot device state and available history. Boop cannot recover a lost key or passphrase.

## Messages, media, and calls

Marmot uses MLS to encrypt chats and groups for members. Nostr relays can store ciphertext and observe connection IPs, timing, event sizes, and routing fields. Relays you authenticate to can learn your public key. Boop uses configured Blossom servers for encrypted attachments and group images. Recipients receive messages and media you send, including location when you choose to share it. Public profile media can also be hosted on Blossom.

Calls use encrypted Nostr signaling and direct WebRTC media between authenticated participants. Peers may learn your network addresses. STUN services receive IP addresses, ports, and request timing to help establish calls. Boop keeps call records locally and does not record call audio or video.

Open Later fetches and verifies public drand beacons. Beacon requests expose connection metadata and the requested round to the service. They contain no message content or identity keys. Loading profile images, link previews, Nostr identifiers, or public files can contact independent websites or media hosts, which can log requests.

If you enable Torrents, resolving a magnet can disclose your IP address and torrent interest to trackers, DHT nodes, and peers. Boop downloads torrent files only after you request them. Metadata resolution follows your plugin settings.

## Permissions and optional processing

- **Microphone and camera.** Used for calls, recording media you choose to send, and scanning address QR codes. An ongoing call may use its active microphone or camera in the background where the operating system permits it.
- **Files and location.** Boop opens files you select, saves media you choose to keep, and accesses your current location when you choose to share it.
- **Bluetooth, notifications, and playback.** Boop uses Bluetooth for audio routing and system controls and notifications for calls, transfers, and playback. Background audio and picture-in-picture let you continue playback outside the app.
- **App Lock.** Device authentication returns a result to Boop. Biometric templates and device credentials stay with the operating system. App Lock adds interface protection only.

Optional chat translation runs on-device with Google ML Kit. The SDK can download models and send usage or performance metrics. [Google ML Kit terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms). Optional voice transcription processes verified local voice notes on-device and stores text and timings in encrypted local storage. Recognizers and language models depend on the device.

## Retention and deletion

Local history and settings stay on the device until removed. Deleting a message can leave a minimal record to prevent replay. You can clear downloads, previews, and transcripts in **Settings → Storage**. Replacing an identity retires the previous account and its app data on the device once the replacement is ready. On Android, clearing app storage removes local app data. Save any backups you intend to keep first.

Recipients, independent relays and media hosts, exports, and operating-system backups may retain their own copies. Their operators control their data and logs. Your chosen destination can keep copies of anything you share or export.

This website uses no tracking scripts or analytics cookies. Its hosting provider may log requests to serve and protect the site. Boop is a general-purpose messenger and is not designed specifically for children.
