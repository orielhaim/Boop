# Boop

Boop is an open-source phone and messenger for Android and iOS. Share your Boop address or scan a QR code to connect. Your address is a Nostr public key, and your device holds the private key. There is no Boop account or phone-number signup.

- Make direct voice calls, invite people into small conferences, and share video with people who choose to watch.
- Send encrypted messages, voice notes, photos, videos, files, and locations. Use Open Later to choose when a message can be opened.
- Manage contacts, block unwanted callers, review message requests, and protect the app with your device lock.
- Translate messages on your device with installed language models. Use Boop in English or Hebrew.

Independent Nostr relays carry encrypted messages and call setup. Encrypted attachments use configurable Blossom servers and download only when you ask. Relays and media servers can observe connection metadata. Direct calls can reveal network addresses to peers.

Boop is a prototype for Android 12 or later and iOS. Keep the app open to receive a call. Some networks prevent direct calls, and delivery depends on relay availability. An encrypted Digital SIM backup lets you restore your address if you lose the device.
